Our Story

Rooted in tradition,
crafted with care

Shanthi Beds was born from a simple belief — that the best sleep comes from nature. For generations, families across Tamil Nadu have trusted ilavam panju (silk cotton) for its unmatched softness, breathability, and purity. We carry that tradition forward, one mattress at a time.

Founded by Shanthi Ravindran in Coimbatore, our workshop on Athipalayam Road is where every mattress and pillow is made entirely by hand. No machines, no shortcuts — just honest craftsmanship using 100% organic silk cotton sourced directly from nature.

We believe your family deserves to sleep on something real. That's why every piece we make is chemical-free, re-stuffable, and built to last for years. From a single pillow to bulk orders, each product leaves our hands with the same care we'd give our own home.

We don't just sell mattresses. We sell the kind of sleep your grandparents had — deep, cool, and completely natural.
